Enable Return YouTube Dislike

To show dislike counts, RYD sends the current video ID and its visible like count to the RYD API.

When you first like or dislike a video, RYD creates a random ID for your extension. Your vote is sent with this ID and the video ID to count likes and dislikes.

Voting does not use your YouTube email, username, or account ID.

After you select Continue, Firefox will ask for permission. If you decline, RYD will not load counts or submit votes. YouTube's own buttons will still work.
